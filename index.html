About the "Lulla n8n" tool

"Lulla n8n" is a private automation tool used only by the owner of this channel. It connects to the channel's own YouTube account to read its performance reports and, in the future, to upload the channel's own videos. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 November 2026

This policy describes the "Lulla n8n" application ("the App"), a private tool operated by the owner of the Lulla & Bloom Kids YouTube channel (contact: lullaandbloomkids@gmail.com). The App is used only by the channel owner to manage their own channel. It is not available to the public.

YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using the App, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. The Google Privacy Policy also applies.

What data the App accesses

  • Channel and video information (titles, publish dates, durations, public view and like counts) of the channel owner's own channel, through the YouTube Data API.
  • Aggregated channel reports (views, watch time, average view duration, traffic sources, thumbnail impressions and click-through rate) through the YouTube Analytics and YouTube Reporting APIs.
  • Video uploads (only if the upload permission is granted): the App uploads the owner's own videos, titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails to the owner's own channel.

The App does not access, collect or store any personal information about viewers, including children. All analytics are aggregated, anonymous statistics provided by YouTube. The App does not use cookies, trackers or advertising identifiers, and this website sets no cookies.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to produce private performance summaries for the channel owner and to publish the owner's own videos.
  • Data is stored only on the channel owner's personal computer. It is not uploaded to any other server.
  • Data is never sold, shared with or disclosed to third parties, and is not used for advertising.
  • OAuth access tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's computer and are used only to call Google APIs.

Limited Use

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Summary files are kept until the owner deletes them. Stored API data is deleted or refreshed at least every 30 days, as required by the YouTube API Services Developer Policies. You can revoke the App's access at any time at Google Account permissions (security settings page); once access is revoked, the App can no longer read or upload anything, and stored data is deleted within 7 days on request.

Children

The channel publishes content made for kids. The App never collects data from or about children or any other viewer.

Changes and contact

Any change to this policy will be published on this page with a new effective date. Questions or deletion requests: lullaandbloomkids@gmail.com.
